About Me

I am Xiaolin Zhou, a PhD student in Information Systems at Arizona State University (ASU), advised by Prof. Xiyang Hu.

Previously, I earned an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Yue Zhao.

My research focuses on LLM agents, reinforcement learning, and reliable AI. I study how agents behave when tools and environments deviate from their assumptions, and how training and evaluation can make these systems more dependable.

I am currently seeking summer 2027 research internships. Please get in touch.
